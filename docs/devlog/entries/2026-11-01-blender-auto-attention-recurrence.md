Thus the October 25 fix worked for shorter turns but stopped suppressing Attention once tool output pushed the settings out of the tail.

The stronger October 29 fixes (`43833dc5`, `c8165050`) existed only on `codex/mcp-auto-review-attention`; neither was an ancestor of main.
