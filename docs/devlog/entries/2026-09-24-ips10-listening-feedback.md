The previous fixed RMS >150 endpoint let background noise prolong captures to twenty or thirty seconds. A bounded audio-owner estimator now takes the lower quintile of 128 pre-wake energy frames, freezes the room floor for that utterance, requires 64 ms of sustained energy, uses a lower continuation threshold, and completes after 700 ms below that threshold. A 180 ms guard excludes the cue from endpoint decisions while preserving the captured samples. No detected speech returns after four seconds; the thirty-second maximum and manual stop remain. Energy measurements remove the per-block DC mean without modifying the waveform sent to the host. This is acoustic endpointing, not a semantic end-of-turn model; unusually long within-sentence pauses and changing background sound remain limits.
