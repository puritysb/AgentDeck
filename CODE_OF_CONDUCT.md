# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

AgentDeck contributors and maintainers are committed to a welcoming,
harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of background, identity,
experience level, or preferred tools and platforms.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Assume good intent while addressing the actual technical or community impact.
- Welcome questions and help newcomers find the relevant documentation.
- Respect privacy, security boundaries, and responsible disclosure.
- Accept maintainer decisions gracefully, including decisions not to merge a
  change.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or unwanted attention.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Deliberate disruption, spam, or repeated bad-faith argument.
- Using project spaces to facilitate unauthorized access or harmful activity.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews,
and other spaces where someone represents the AgentDeck project.

For a routine concern, flag the relevant content to a maintainer. For a sensitive
report, contact the maintainer privately using the contact method on the
[maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/puritysb). Do not publish private
details in an issue.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close or lock conversations, reject
contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when needed.
They will seek a response proportionate to the behavior and its impact.
